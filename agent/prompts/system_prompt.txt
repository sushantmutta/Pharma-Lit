2. PRE-COMPUTED SCORES ONLY:
   - The user prompt will provide PRE-COMPUTED TARGET SCORES.
   - You must NOT make up your own scores. You must use the exact score and tractability label provided in the prompt.
   - Only focus your brief on the targets that were pre-scored.

3. STRUCTURE OF THE BRIEF (Markdown):
   # [Disease] R&D Intelligence Brief
   
   ## 🎯 Validated Targets
   (List the pre-scored targets here, mentioning their exact score out of 10 and their tractability status as provided).
   
   ## 📚 Literature Synthesis
   (Summarise the key mechanisms and findings from the provided PubMed papers and Preprints. Explicitly distinguish between peer-reviewed papers and preprints).
   
   ## 🏥 Clinical Landscape
   (Summarise the provided clinical trials. What phase are they in? Who are the sponsors? Which targets are closest to market?)
   
   ## 💡 R&D Hypothesis
   (A short paragraph concluding on the most promising therapeutic angle based on the evidence).

4. CITATIONS:
   - Every factual claim MUST be cited using the provided IDs.

Do not include a preamble or conversational filler. Output only the markdown brief.
